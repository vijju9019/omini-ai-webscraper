Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 • Compliant with Microsoft Edge Add-ons & Chrome Web Store Policies

1. Commitment to Privacy

OmniScrape AI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. OmniScrape AI is built on a 100% client-side architecture. We do not operate backend servers that capture, log, or transmit your extracted web data or browsing habits.

2. Data Collection & Processing

OmniScrape AI does not collect, transmit, or monetize any personal data.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Remote Web Tracking: We do not track your browsing history or retain logs of the websites you visit.
  • Local Execution Only: When you initiate extraction, content parsing and element clustering occur entirely in your local browser memory via JavaScript.
  • Exported Datasets: Datasets you export (CSV, Excel XLSX, JSON, TSV) are generated locally in memory and downloaded directly to your local file system.

3. Manifest Permissions and Justifications

In accordance with Microsoft Edge and Chrome Web Store requirements, below is a transparent justification for every browser permission requested:

Permission Justification
activeTab Allows OmniScrape AI to inspect and scrape the HTML elements on the currently active tab only when you interact with the extension.
scripting Required to inject DOM element detection and visual selector highlights on the target webpage you choose to scrape.
storage Used solely to store your local preferences (such as field column configurations and pagination limits) on your device via chrome.storage.local.
downloads Required to trigger saving your exported CSV, Excel (.xlsx), and JSON files to your local Downloads directory.
sidePanel Enables the user interface to dock alongside the target webpage in Edge and Chrome without obscuring web page content.
tabs Used to coordinate side-panel state with active browser navigation and pagination actions across pages.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

OmniScrape AI contains no third-party tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or affiliate trackers.

5. Compliance with Laws

Because we do not store, process, or transmit personal data on external servers, OmniScrape AI is inherently compliant with major privacy regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the security of OmniScrape AI, please submit an issue or inquiry through our GitHub repository or contact the extension support desk.
