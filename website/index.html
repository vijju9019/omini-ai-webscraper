Key Capabilities

Everything you need to gather high-quality training datasets, competitor intelligence, and market listings.

Intelligent Pattern Clustering

Our sibling DOM similarity algorithm groups recurring cards, list items, and tabular layouts automatically with sub-second accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

Is OmniScrape AI completely free to use?
Yes! OmniScrape AI is 100% free with no hidden credit systems, paywalls, or row limitations. Because data extraction runs entirely inside your browser using client-side JavaScript, there are no expensive server costs passed to you.
Does it work on websites with dynamic JavaScript and Infinite Scroll?
Yes. OmniScrape AI features automated mutation settling and dynamic scrolling that stimulates `IntersectionObserver` triggers, allowing it to seamlessly scrape Single-Page Applications (React, Vue, Angular), infinite scroll feeds, and AJAX-loaded catalogs.
Where is my scraped data stored?
Your data is stored exclusively in your local browser memory and downloaded directly to your computer. We do not operate remote tracking servers or collect your data.
What export formats are supported?
You can export to Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) with auto-formatted column headers, CSV with standard UTF-8 Byte Order Mark (BOM) to prevent character distortion in spreadsheets, clean JSON, or direct clipboard copy (TSV) for instant pasting into Google Sheets.
